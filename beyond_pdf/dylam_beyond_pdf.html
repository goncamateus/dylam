DyLam Beyond-PDF Companion — ChickenBanana λ-simplex training scrubber

DyLam decomposes a multi-objective reward into per-Component critics and adapts a weight λ(t) over training to trade them off. Scrub the slider below to watch λ move through training on ChickenBanana and see the greedy policy it induces at each moment. Click anywhere in the weight simplex to try a different, fixed weighting against the same learned Component Q-tables at that same moment — the counterfactual this page exists to make checkable.

Every frame on this page was computed offline in Python from real checkpoints of a training run reported in the paper, and only looked up here — the browser recomputes nothing about the method. Every policy shown is greedy, with no exploration: it is not the behaviour used while training, only the behaviour that training's Q-tables currently imply.
